ABOUT

From reusable cups on campus to neighbourhood climate gatherings.

Hi, I’m Samar.

I co-led a reusable cup-sharing program at UBC and later helped bring neighbours together for a climate block party in Vancouver. Both involved giving people a way to take part in something close to home.

Over five years, I’ve worked on digital marketing, events and political advocacy with businesses, public-sector projects, B Corps, non-profits and student organizations.

I enjoy making complex topics easier to get into, whether I’m filming a reel, writing an email or helping people find their way into a conversation.
