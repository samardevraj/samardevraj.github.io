The event

The block party was a capstone project for The Starfish Canada’s Climate75 program. We invited neighbours, artists, musicians, community organizations and local elected officials to gather ahead of BC’s municipal elections.

It was free, family-friendly and non-partisan. The idea was to give people a place to talk about local climate concerns while enjoying a day with their neighbours.

The communications

I co-led the event and worked across its announcement, reels, partner introductions and recap. Our invitation opened with music, art and a day with neighbours, then connected the gathering to climate conversations with local elected officials.

From the invitation“come soak in the sun and say hi to your neighbours!”From the block party invitation

The My Climate Plan collaboration made that connection practical: interviews about Vancouver’s pedestrian zone led into a story about how knowing your neighbours helps communities respond to crises. It ended with an invitation to the block party.
